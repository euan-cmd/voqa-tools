How to Use This Calculator

EV charging helps hotels in two ways: attracting new guests who won't stay without charging, and retaining existing guests who might otherwise switch to a competitor that has it.
Set your target payback period and the calculator will tell you how many EV guest room nights per month you need — from new bookings and retained guests combined — to justify your investment.
